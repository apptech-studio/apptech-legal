Privacy Policy

Moto Doc Inspector ("the app", "we") helps motorbike dealers capture, extract and cross-check vehicle paperwork. This policy describes the data we process, why, and your rights. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Data on your device

The business data you enter — vehicle records (chassis/engine numbers, plate, model, prices and other fields), captured document images, drafts, revenue and history — is stored locally on your device (offline-first). This data is not sent to our servers. Any backup you create is kept on your device or your own cloud storage, which we cannot access.

2. Data we collect / process

Data typePurpose
A device identifierIdentifies your device to manage feature usage limits and activate licenses. The app does not require you to create an account.
Usage data (screen views, taps, scan/extract counts)Anonymous analytics to improve the app.
Diagnostics (crash reports, performance)To detect and fix problems.
Document images (only when you use AI extraction)When you actively scan a document, the image is sent to a third-party AI service to recognize the fields. See sections 4 and 5.

3. Camera and photos

The app needs camera access to capture documents and photo library access to open existing images. Images are kept on your device unless you actively use the AI extraction feature (section 4).

4. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party providers for AI image extraction (to read fields from invoices, VNeID and factory-exit slips), analytics, crash reporting and remote configuration. When you use AI extraction, the relevant document image is sent to the AI provider for processing on their infrastructure under their own privacy terms. We send only the image you chose to process, only for the extraction you requested.

5. Documents containing personal data

The documents you scan may contain personal information of third parties (for example names, national ID numbers from VNeID, addresses). You, the dealer/business using the app, are the controller of that data. You are responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consent to collect, store and process it. We process these images only to extract and reconcile the fields you request; we do not use them to build advertising profiles.

6. In-app purchases

If the app offers paid plans or one-time purchases, payments are handled by Apple through your App Store account. We do not collect or store any payment details.

7. Your rights

  • Delete all local data by removing records in the app or uninstalling it.
  • Decline tracking so any ads are not personalized.
  • Manage or cancel any subscription in your device's App Store settings.
  • Contact us about your data using the email below.

8. Children

Moto Doc Inspector is a business tool intended for adult professionals and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
