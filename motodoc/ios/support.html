Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document?

Open a vehicle, choose the document type (invoice, VNeID, or factory-exit slip) and point the camera at it, or pick an image from your library. The app extracts the fields and cross-checks them across the three sources.

Why do you need camera and photo library access?

The camera captures documents; the photo library lets you open existing images. Images stay on your device unless you actively use AI extraction.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Vehicle records and images are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers. Only when you actively use AI extraction is a document image sent to a third-party AI service to read the fields. See our Privacy Policy.

The AI read a field incorrectly — what do I do?

AI extraction is assistive. Every extracted field can be edited manually before you save. Always review important fields (chassis/engine numbers, ID numbers) against the original document.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

If you bought a plan, open device Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions → Moto Doc Inspector. Cancel before the period ends to avoid renewal.

I got a new phone — how do I restore my purchases?

Open the plans screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your plan is restored via the Apple ID used to buy it.
